tekst_en: |
  Spang Makandra means "eating together" in Surinamese, and that's exactly what has been happening at Rozengracht 214 since 1978.

  This is one of Amsterdam's oldest Surinamese eateries, and it remains a go-to for regulars who come back for the classics: roti, saoto soup, moksi meti, nasi goreng, and bami. The kitchen reflects what Suriname has always been: a country of many influences: Creole, Hindustani, Javanese, Chinese, and European, all recognisable in what arrives at your table.

  On the Rozengracht, a street that has everything, Spang Makandra is one of the places that has simply always been there and belongs.

  Open for a quick lunch or a full evening meal. Monday–Saturday 11:00–22:00, Sunday 13:00–22:00. Reservations at spangmakandra.nl.
