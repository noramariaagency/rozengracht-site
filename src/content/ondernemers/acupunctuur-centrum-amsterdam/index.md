  Xiu Deng grew up surrounded by needles and herbs. Her grandfather was an acupuncturist, her mother too, and she is the third generation practising traditional Chinese medicine, now from her practice at Rozengracht 53 in Amsterdam.

The practice covers a wide range of conditions: chronic pain, stress, sleep disorders, hormonal complaints, and recovery after illness or surgery.

  The Rozengracht practice has become a quiet space in a busy city, found by Amsterdam residents and visitors who are looking for an alternative or a complement to conventional medicine.
