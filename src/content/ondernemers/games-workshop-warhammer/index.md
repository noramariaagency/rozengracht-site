tekst_en: |
  Warhammer Amsterdam West, at Rozengracht 99, is the Amsterdam branch of Games Workshop, the British company behind the Warhammer miniatures game.

  The store is run by manager Ramon Laan. Staff behind the counter play the games themselves, and customers particularly mention the help they get with painting techniques, whether they're picking up a brush for the first time or have been collecting for years.

  Wedged between clothing shops and cafés on the Rozengracht, one of Amsterdam's main shopping streets in the Jordaan, it's one of the few addresses on the street where you leave with an army instead of groceries.

  Inside are the full Warhammer ranges, paint and brushes, plus room to sit down and paint or play a game on the spot. Open Tuesday to Saturday, with a midday break; closed Mondays and Sundays.
