tekst_en: |
  The Uzcudun brothers opened Salmuera on the Rozengracht in 2015 as the first mezcaleria in the Netherlands. Since then, they have built one of the most extensive mezcal collections in Europe, with more than 150 bottles on the menu.

  The restaurant brings together Argentine asado from a charcoal grill, Peruvian ceviche, and Latin American street food in one room: open kitchen, dark wood, candles, and counter seating at the bar. The bife ancho ribeye in chipotle beso sauce, the ceviche classico with sea bass and leche de tigre, and the whole suckling pig over an open fire are reasons people keep coming back. The family connection runs deep: their father, restaurateur Jorge Uzcudun, has been part of Amsterdam's restaurant scene for decades and runs Paso Doble on the Westerstraat.

  On the Rozengracht, Salmuera found the right home: a street big enough for a restaurant that stands out, but neighbourly enough that you'd go on a Tuesday just because you feel like it.
