  Fabula Rosa – Rozengracht 15, Amsterdam

  Three floors of crystals, tarot cards, and quiet. Right on the Rozengracht.

  Fabula Rosa at Rozengracht 15 is one of Amsterdam's most extensive spiritual shops. Walk in and you'll find gemstones and crystals in every size, polished and raw, alongside one of the city's largest collections of tarot and oracle cards. There's also incense, palo santo, sage, salt lamps, meditation cushions, and singing bowls. Spread across three floors, in an unhurried atmosphere where you can browse at your own pace.

  The shop has been a fixture on the Rozengracht for years and has become a regular stop for people looking for something specific, such as a particular stone or a particular deck, and for those who wander in for the first time. Fabula Rosa also works with publisher Schors for spiritual and esoteric books.

  Open Tuesday to Saturday, usually from 12:00 to 17:00. Call ahead on +31 20 638 2505.
