tekst_en: |
  At Rozengracht 214 the lights come on late in the afternoon and stay on until the small hours.

  Eethuis Ali Baba is a Turkish place built around meat off the spit: shawarma and doner, in a sandwich or a durum wrap, or as kapsalon with fries underneath. The menu also runs to lahmacun and Turkish pizza, with soup, salads and sutlac for dessert. Everything is halal.

  This end of the Rozengracht, a long shopping street in Amsterdam's Jordaan, is where the street stays open in the evening, and Ali Baba is where you end up once the rest has closed. Neighbours, taxi drivers and people heading back from the centre all sit here together.

  You can eat in or take it away, and the delivery apps carry the menu too. A lamb shawarma sandwich runs to about eleven euros, a kapsalon a little more.

  Stop by at Rozengracht 214 in Amsterdam, somewhere between dinner and the end of the night.
