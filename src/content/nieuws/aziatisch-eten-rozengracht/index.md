tekst_en: |
  Looking for Asian food in Amsterdam? You do not have to cross the city for it. The Rozengracht, one of the main streets running west out of the Jordaan district, has five kitchens within a few minutes' walk of each other, from Thailand and Japan to Vietnam and Bali.

  Dong Son serves Thai dishes to take away, which makes it an easy stop when you want something good in a hurry.

  Warie's Thai Food is about authentic Thai cooking, prepared fresh in an open kitchen. Pad Thai, green curry, Tom Kha Kai and spicy salads all show how varied Thai food can be.

  If you would rather eat Japanese, Akitsu has an extensive menu. There is sushi and sashimi, of course, but also yakitori, teriyaki and other warm Japanese dishes.

  For Vietnamese flavours, there is Bánh Mì Bà Mỹ, where the baguettes are made fresh. The kitchen also does Vietnamese noodle and rice dishes, alongside house-made drinks.

  And then there is Long Pura, built around the rich cooking of Bali: fragrant rice tables, spiced satay and authentic Balinese dishes.

  Five addresses, several kitchens and plenty of choice. A quick Thai meal, a plate of sushi, a fresh banh mi or a full Balinese rice table: for anyone who likes Asian food, there is a lot on this one street.
