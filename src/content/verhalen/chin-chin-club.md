tekst_en: |
  There's no cross on the facade of Rozengracht 133 anymore. Yet this building has a past that goes back much further than most Chin Chin Club visitors would guess.

  The building opened in 1929 as the Roman Catholic association hall "Joannes Roothaan," named after the Amsterdam-born Jan Roothaan, who became the highest official of the Jesuit order in 1829. Architect H.W. Valk designed it, along with the neighbouring De Zaaier church.

  In 1955, a talent contest here crowned Johnny Jordaan and Tante Leen as the Best Voice of the Jordaan. When De Zaaier closed in the 1970s, the Roothaanhuis lost its Catholic role too. It became a community centre hosting student parties, billiards tournaments and band nights. In 1999, the Balkan Peace Coalition held its founding meeting there.

  Around 2000, Club More opened in the building, with house music and Rewind Parties for the crowd left behind when the Roxy closed. In 2013, fish restaurant Pesca moved in, and in 2017 Chin Chin Club claimed the basement below it: a raw space converted into restaurant, karaoke bar and nightclub at once.

  From Jesuits to karaoke, in under a century. More info via chinchinclub.nl.
