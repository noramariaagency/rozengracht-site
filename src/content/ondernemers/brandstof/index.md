tekst_en: |
  Bar Brandstof on the corner of the Rozengracht and Marnixstraat (nr. 357) is one of the neighbourhood's fixed points, open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, with a menu that shifts with the seasons every quarter.

  Chefs Monto Joris and Varen van de Ende cooked here for years for a regular crowd of locals and for passers-by drawn in off the street. House-made granola, fresh sandwiches on farmers' bread, and the Fuel Burger with jalapeño mayo are the dishes people remember. On the Rozengracht, a street that moves all day, Brandstof was the place to sit down.
