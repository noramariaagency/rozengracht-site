  Tsering and Tsundue brought Tibetan Kunye massage to the Jordaan.

  In April 2021, Tsering Yangzom and Tsundue Sangmo opened their studio at Rozengracht 7 in Amsterdam. Both trained in Tibetan Kunye massage. This is an ancient technique that combines warm herbal oils, acupressure, and rhythmic movement to ease tension in both body and mind. Not a wellness trend, but a method rooted in traditional Tibetan medicine.

  Alongside massages, TARA also offers nail treatments, making it a complete stop on the Rozengracht for anyone looking to unwind properly. The studio is open seven days a week, from 11:00 to 21:00 (Friday until 22:00).
