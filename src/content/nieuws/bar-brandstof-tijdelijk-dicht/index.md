tekst_en: |
  Bar Brandstof, on the corner of the Rozengracht and Marnixstraat, is temporarily closed. The ongoing construction work on the Rozengracht, Marnixstraat and the nearby bridge made things unworkable for the business: fences, a dug-up street and detours left too little passing trade.

  The team hopes to reopen in 2027, once the works are finished. Until then Bar Brandstof stays closed; more information is available at bar-brandstof.nl.
