tekst_en: |
  A few doors from the Westerkerk, at Rozengracht 1A, sits a coffeeshop that stands out for what's missing: no tourist neon, just reggae on the speakers and two floors with distinct moods of their own.

  African Blackstar is one of the few Black-owned coffeeshops in Amsterdam. Upstairs there's a bar with a TV screen, downstairs a smoking lounge with its own soundtrack. The menu runs to dozens of products, from Californian strains like Gelato and Amnesia Haze to Moroccan hash, pre-rolled joints and a few edibles for anyone taking it slower.

  On the Rozengracht, a short walk from the Anne Frank House and the Prinsengracht, the shop draws in locals along with visitors who've just left the crowds near the Nieuwmarkt behind.

  Inside there are bench seats, a flatscreen and staff who know the regulars by name. Card payment is accepted, and solo visitors are just as welcome as groups.

  It's open daily from 11am until late.
