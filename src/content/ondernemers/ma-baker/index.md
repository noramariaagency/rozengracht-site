tekst_en: |
  On the corner where the Rozengracht runs into the Marnixstraat sits a snack bar every local in Amsterdam knows: Ma Baker.

  Run by the Hefny brothers, it serves the menu you'd expect from a proper Amsterdam snack bar: sandwiches, falafel, pita, frikandellen and croquettes, fresh from the display case all day. No decor, no fuss: food that's ready before you've finished ordering.

  Walk from the Rozengracht toward the Marnixstraat and you'll pass it without trying; this corner is one of the busiest crossings in Amsterdam-West, full of shoppers by day and bar-goers by night.

  Ma Baker stays open daily, often long after the rest of the street has pulled down its shutters.

  Stop in on your way home. After a night out in the Jordaan, this is the shortest route to something hot.
