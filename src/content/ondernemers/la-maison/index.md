  Brigitte van den Dungen-Schreurs opened a hair salon at Rozengracht 215 in August 2012 and called it La Maison.

  She saw room for an affordable salon bringing specialists from different fields under one roof, and has run it since according to the Total Beauty Concept of the French brand La Biosthétique. Martin van den Dungen-Schreurs is also involved in running the business.

  On the Rozengracht, at the edge of the Jordaan near the Westertoren, La Maison marked its tenth anniversary in 2022.

  Stylists at La Maison cut, colour and fit hair extensions, and treat scalp issues such as dandruff and hair loss. A beauty side offers facials and skin treatments including Thermage FLX and Fraxel. All products come from La Biosthétique, whose flagship store in Amsterdam this is.

  Walk-ins for a haircut are welcome, or book ahead through the website.
