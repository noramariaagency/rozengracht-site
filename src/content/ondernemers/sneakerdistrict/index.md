  Sneakerdistrict sells most of its shoes online, but if you want to try a pair on before you buy it, you go to Rozengracht 21. It is the company's only physical store.

  The shelves hold models from Asics, adidas, New Balance and Karhu, alongside the releases that move just as quickly in the webshop. The store is also a stockist for On, where you can arrange a time to try running shoes and running apparel without being rushed.

  The Rozengracht makes sense for a shop like this. It is one of the main shopping streets in the Jordaan in Amsterdam, full of people who were not necessarily out looking for sneakers that day. On weekdays the store is open from 11:00 to 19:00, with different hours at the weekend.

  Walk in, name the model you saw online and ask whether they have it in your size.
