tekst_en: |
  My moved to the Netherlands with her husband in 1993 and spent years cooking Vietnamese food here for family and friends. On 26 August 2025 she opened her own place at Rozengracht 239.

  She grew up in Ho Chi Minh City and learned to cook from her grandmother, which is where the name comes from: Bà Mỹ means grandma My. The baguettes for the banh mi are baked on the spot and filled with pulled chicken, fried pork belly or tofu. The menu also carries pho, com tam with grilled meat, and bun bo Hue. There are vegetarian options, and fish dishes too.

  This stretch of the Rozengracht, a shopping street on the edge of Amsterdam's Jordaan, was home to Ben Cohen Tel-Aviv Streetfood and later Amori Pizza. Now there are bamboo hats on the wall and wicker rickshaws in the room, and the street has a family-run Vietnamese kitchen.

  Prices sit between 6.95 and 10.95 euros, so a sandwich on the way past works as well as a full meal. Regulars come back for the bun bo Hue.

  Walk in at Rozengracht 239 in Amsterdam, or call 020-3031057.
