tekst_en: |
  A quick bite, a long lunch, or an afternoon that quietly turns into a few hours on a terrace? On the Rozengracht, one of the main shopping streets in Amsterdam's Jordaan district, all three are within a few minutes' walk.

  At Pesca, everything revolves around fish. You pick your own fish and side dishes from the fish counter, and the kitchen prepares it for you. It makes for a lunch that is a little different from the usual.

  At Kessens you can take your time. The menu includes fresh sandwiches, croissants, soups and a range of classics. And when the sun is out, there is a terrace on the street.

  Looking for something quick? Broodje Mokum has what you need: a good sandwich to take with you, or to eat at an unhurried pace on the terrace.

  Then there is Brandstof, whose large terrace catches the sun on clear days. Good to know, though: Bar Brandstof is temporarily closed at the moment because of the construction work on the Rozengracht and Marnixstraat, with reopening expected in 2027.

  Four addresses, four different moods. And each of them a good reason to step out of the day for a while around lunchtime.
