tekst_en: |
  There is charcoal smoke coming off Rozengracht 5 again, these days under the name Uno Mas.

  Hany runs an Argentinian steak restaurant in the spot that housed Amigo Grill for years. The menu is built around the grill: steaks, spare ribs that come off the bone easily, plus a handful of seafood dishes and salads for anyone who wants something lighter. Guests keep coming back to the rib-eye and the lamb chops, and to the chimichurri that arrives without you having to ask.

  The Rozengracht is one of Amsterdam's busy shopping streets in the Jordaan, the kind of street people walk down on their way somewhere else. Uno Mas is where they stop. Locals from the neighbourhood sit next to visitors who have just come from the Westerkerk tower around the corner.

  The restaurant is open every day from 12:00 to 23:00, so it works for a quick lunch as well as a long dinner. Call 020 638 62 64 to book a table, or simply walk in and see what is free.
