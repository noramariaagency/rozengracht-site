tekst_en: |
  In 1975, Rietje and Guillaume Nivard opened what became the oldest specialist children's bookshop in the Netherlands. It is also the second oldest in the world.

  They ran it on Rozengracht 34 for nearly forty years before retiring in 2014. Publisher De Wakkere Muis took over, because letting a place like this disappear seemed like a waste. That decision is kept alive every day by staff who know seven thousand titles and have read almost all of them. Ask for a book for a six-year-old who loves dinosaurs and they'll know exactly which shelf to point you to.

  After more than fifty years at number 34, the shop moved in 2026 to a bigger space across the street, at Rozengracht 101-103, with more room for the collection and its own coffee corner.

  Alongside books you'll find stuffed animals, games, and other creative things for children. De Kinderboekwinkel is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 to 18:00.

  Rozengracht 101-103, Amsterdam. Call +31 20 622 4761 or email info@kinderboekwinkel.nl.
