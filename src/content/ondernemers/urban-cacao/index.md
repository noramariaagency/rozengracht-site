tekst_en: |
  Urban Cacao opened on the Rozengracht in 2014 and brought something most chocolate shops don't offer: origin chocolate from more than 40 growing regions, made from beans you can trace back to a specific place.

  Around 80% of the cacao comes from South America — Venezuela, Peru, Brazil, the Dominican Republic — with additional beans from Asia and Africa. Alongside the single-origin bars, there are blended tablets with stroopwafel, sea salt caramel, matcha, almonds, hazelnuts, and more. Truffles and macarons complete the range. The shop has more than 200 recipes to discover in-store, and tasting is encouraged.
