tekst_en: |
  The Uzcudun brothers, half-Argentine, with a father who has been a fixture in Amsterdam's restaurant world for decades, opened Chamuyero as a tribute to the delicatessens of Buenos Aires, right next door to their restaurant Salmuera on the Rozengracht.

  Every day the counter fills up with freshly baked medialunas, empanadas, and alfajores. The deli also carries fresh cuts of Argentine Black Angus beef sourced directly from Salmuera, alongside Latin American drinks. "Chamuyero" is Argentine street slang for someone who can sell with words. Here, the food does the talking.

  Together with Salmuera and Sins of Sal, Chamuyero is part of La Familia Amsterdam, the Uzcudun brothers' small hospitality family on the Rozengracht. Three different concepts, one shared story.

  Come in for breakfast, lunch, or a late-afternoon snack. Open Wednesday–Sunday 11:00–17:00. Also available via Uber Eats and Thuisbezorgd. Find them at Rozengracht 104H and at chamuyero.nl.
