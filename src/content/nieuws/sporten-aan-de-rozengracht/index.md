tekst_en: |
  The Rozengracht, one of Amsterdam's main shopping streets in the Jordaan, is not the first place you would look for a workout, but a short stretch of it holds a surprising range of options: yoga, Pilates, personal training and high intensity workouts, all within a few hundred metres.

  At K/C Move, at Rozengracht 93A, the idea is to move smartly and with attention. Its KinetiCode method combines Pilates, strength work and functional training. You can join group classes, book personal training or train in duet sessions. Whether you want to get stronger, work on your posture or prepare for a specific sporting goal, there are several ways to start.

  A few doors along, at Rozengracht 191, is Equal Yoga. Alongside various styles of yoga, the schedule includes Pilates, mobility and sculpt classes. From a quiet Yin class to Power Flow, Hot Pilates or Hot Sculpt, you decide how gentle or how demanding your session is. The studio has two rooms, showers and changing facilities.

  If you would rather train one to one, Ultimate Performance at Rozengracht 207 offers personal guidance and a programme built entirely around your own goals. No large groups, just individual coaching and focused training.

  And for anyone who prefers to go all out, there is RUSH at Rozengracht 88. In 50-minute workouts you alternate treadmill running with strength stations: sprints, sled pushes and dumbbell work. You can choose between RUSH lower, RUSH upper and RUSH full. Afterwards there is the Recovery Zone, with compression boots and massage equipment among other things.

  From gentle movement to a serious sweat, the Rozengracht has something to match most sporting ambitions.
