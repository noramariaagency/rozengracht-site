tekst_en: |
  This month TOPCHIRO Amsterdam marks five years at Rozengracht 217, on the Rozengracht, one of the main shopping streets in Amsterdam's Jordaan district. Five years as part of the street, and by now a familiar face on it.

  TOPCHIRO specialises in chiropractic care and works with people who have complaints in their back, neck and shoulders, among others. The practice also offers guidance to anyone who wants to work preventively on posture, movement and freedom of movement. The team is made up of chiropractors and chiropractic advisers, and it regularly organises health talks and shares practical health tips.

  With locations across the Netherlands, TOPCHIRO has grown into a large organisation. The Amsterdam practice has been on the Rozengracht for five years now, and hopefully for many more. Congratulations, TOPCHIRO!
