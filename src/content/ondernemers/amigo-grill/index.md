  Halim knows exactly how you want your steak.

  At Amigo Grill, it comes down to meat, fire, and the Argentine hospitality that owner Halim has been bringing to the Rozengracht for years. The parrillada, a platter of charcoal-grilled meats, is the undisputed highlight of the menu, but Halim is equally known for his honest wine recommendations. He takes his time with every table, asks how you want your steak done, and thinks along about what works together.

  Amigo Grill has held its spot at Rozengracht 5, in the middle of Amsterdam's Jordaan neighbourhood, long enough to earn a loyal local following that keeps coming back. The restaurant is open from noon until midnight, making it a solid choice for both lunch and a long, relaxed dinner.
