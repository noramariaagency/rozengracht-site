tekst_en: |
  Sergey Shevelev brought his lifelong interest in Asian tea culture to Amsterdam and opened the Netherlands' first authentic tea culture club at Rozengracht 92H: Moychay.

  The tea house has three distinct rooms, Japanese, Chinese, and Taiwanese, each designed as a proper place to sit, taste, and experience tea ceremony. The range covers more than 250 types of tea from 13 countries, alongside more than 1,000 teaware items for the Chinese Gong Fu Cha ceremony. One of the rooms can be reserved for a private Ping Cha tea tasting or a Gong Fu Cha ceremony, for yourself or a small group.

  The Rozengracht gave Moychay the right setting: a street with enough character and diversity to accommodate something that asks you to slow down.

  You can also walk in for a cup of tea at the bar, or order online at togo.moychay.nl. Contact: +31 6 25395868.
