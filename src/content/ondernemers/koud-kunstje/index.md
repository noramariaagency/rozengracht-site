tekst_en: |
  At Koud Kunstje on the Rozengracht, you can buy handmade jewellery, ceramics, and candles, or make them yourself.

  The shop doubles as a creative workshop space. Alongside original handcrafted products, Koud Kunstje runs guided workshops in jewellery making, drawing, candle casting, and other creative techniques. The instructors walk you through every step, so no experience is needed. For children's birthday parties, groups of up to 10 kids (ages 5 and up) can book a full guided workshop at €25 per child.

  The Rozengracht location fits the shop perfectly: the street draws a mix of locals, expats, and curious visitors who are open to discovering something a little different.

  Adults can also book individual sessions or short courses at the workshop atelier on nearby Geuzenstraat 59. Check the website for the current workshop schedule, or walk in during opening hours.

  Opening hours: Tuesday–Friday 12:00–18:00, Saturday 11:00–17:00.
