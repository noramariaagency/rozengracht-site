tekst_en: |
  From the street, Akitsu's shopfront on Rozengracht 228 gives away almost nothing. Step inside and you're in one of Amsterdam's longest-running Japanese restaurants.

  Chef Hiroshi Konishi and the Chow family have run Akitsu for more than three decades; daughter Isabelle Chow works the floor and builds the wine and sake list herself, matching each pour to what's on the plate. The menu covers sushi, sashimi, tempura and grilled dishes, served in a room finished in bamboo and lacquered wood, with no background music to get in the way.

  On the Rozengracht, one of Amsterdam's main shopping streets in the Jordaan, Akitsu barely stands out among the shops and cafés, which is exactly how its regulars like it.

  Book by phone; the kitchen is open Tuesday to Saturday evenings only, from 6pm to 10pm, with takeaway available from 4:30pm to 9pm.

  Go on a quiet Tuesday and you'll see why the menu hasn't needed to change much.
