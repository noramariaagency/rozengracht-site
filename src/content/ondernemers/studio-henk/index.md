tekst_en: |
  Okke and Xander Albers started Studio HENK in 2013 with a table they built themselves from reclaimed wood salvaged from Amsterdam's canal houses. Ten years later, their flagship store is at Rozengracht 204-210: almost 1,000 m² across three floors, in an 18th-century building that once housed the Lucas Bols distillery and a coffee syrup factory.

  Studio HENK makes furniture to order — configured entirely to the customer's preferences in size, material, fabric, and colour. Everything is produced in the Netherlands or elsewhere in Europe. Studio HENK is B Corp certified and is working toward a fully zero-waste production model. The showroom displays the complete collection, with complimentary interior advice available.

  Choosing the Rozengracht fits what Studio HENK wants to be: present in the city where they started, on a street big enough for a store of this size.
