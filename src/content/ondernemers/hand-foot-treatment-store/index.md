tekst_en: |
  Angelique Piso has worked in the beauty industry for more than twenty years and has run her own business since 2012, now based at Rozengracht 77A in Amsterdam.

  Piso deliberately calls her place a hand and foot spa rather than a nail studio: the treatments focus on the health of the nail, not just the polish on top. She works with cosmetics that are environmentally and animal friendly, and clients can take the products home with them.

  On the Rozengracht, among the shops and cafés of the Jordaan, the store offers a quiet spot away from the bustle of the street outside.

  There's no cutting or dyeing here, just manicures and pedicures, in a setting closer to a spa than an average nail bar. Piso keeps the number of clients per day limited, leaving room for actual conversation.

  It's open Tuesday through Saturday, by appointment, for manicures and pedicures.
