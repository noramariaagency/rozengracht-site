tekst_en: |
  Rembrandt van Rijn spent the last years of his life at Rozengracht 184. He died there on 4 October 1669. Today the building houses a 220 m² tattoo studio and art gallery, operating since 2013.

  Antiek Tattoo works with an international team of artists specialising in a range of styles: fine line, traditional, Japanese, realism, and more. The studio is large enough for several artists to work simultaneously and considered enough to give each client individual attention. Walk-ins are welcome for smaller designs: no weeks-long waiting lists. Larger and detailed work is done by appointment. The building is part of the Rembrandt trail on the Rozengracht.

  When you put a tattoo studio somewhere, it helps to find a building with history. They found it.
