tekst_en: |
  Casper Holtkamp grew up around the ovens of Holtkamp, Amsterdam's well-known pastry and croquette family, and opened his own restaurant on Rozengracht 24 at the end of 2014, together with his wife Anna.

  Anna moved from Stockholm to the Netherlands in 2002, and that Scandinavian background shows up on the menu next to recipes from the Holtkamp family. By day it's toast Skagen with shrimp and dill, warm-smoked salmon, and croquettes from Casper's own patisserie.

  On the Rozengracht, one of Amsterdam's main shopping streets in the Jordaan, Kessens is a quiet stop between the trams and the shop windows, with white tables and a display case full of pastries.

  The menu runs from French to Scandinavian: toast Skagen, eggs with anchovy mayonnaise, crêpes Suzette, and a steak sandwich with caramelised shallot. The display case holds pastries from Holtkamp, starting at 5 euros.

  Kessens opens at 8:30 on weekdays and 9:00 on weekends, for breakfast and lunch.
