tekst_en: |
  Warie's Thai Food has been at the corner of the Rozengracht and Marnixstraat since 2001. This makes it the oldest Thai restaurant in Amsterdam.

  Suphawadi "Lekkie" Anorat took over the restaurant in 2012 and cooks fresh Thai dishes every evening in an open kitchen. Guests can watch their food being prepared. The menu is authentic and the portions are generous. Because seating is limited, reservations are strongly recommended. The restaurant fills up regularly.

  On the Rozengracht, where there's no shortage of places to eat, Warie's is the kind of spot where regulars don't need to look at the menu. They already know what they want.

  Open Monday–Thursday 16:30–22:00, Friday 16:30–22:30. Rozengracht 235, Amsterdam. Phone 020 622 36 38. Delivery via Uber Eats. More at wariethaifood.nl.
