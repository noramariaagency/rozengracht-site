tekst_en: |
  On the Rozengracht in Amsterdam, one of the city's main shopping streets in the Jordaan, Vélo runs its rides in the dark, lit by candles and driven by music.

  Classes run 45 or 60 minutes: the beginner-friendly V45, the punishing Véloh My God, a strength-focused V-Strength and a meditative V-Meditate. Instructors pick their own music and mood for each ride. Vélo describes itself as woman-owned and LGBTQ-friendly, and runs a second Amsterdam studio in De Pijp alongside the Rozengracht location.

  On a stretch of the Rozengracht that's picked up more fitness and beauty studios in recent years, Vélo fits right in between the shops and cafés.

  Cycling shoes are provided and cleaned between classes; there are showers, towels, and a bar where you can pre-order a shake for after your ride.

  Book through the app: the door locks once class starts, so arriving on time isn't a suggestion.
