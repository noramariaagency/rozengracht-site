tekst_en: |
  At Dong Son you order at the counter, and ten minutes later you walk back out onto the Rozengracht with a warm parcel under your arm.

  Dwayne runs a Thai take-away at number 22 with a short menu, and the short menu is the whole idea. The crispy pork belly is what brings people back: tender meat, skin that actually cracks. Alongside it there is khao soi, the northern Thai noodle curry you rarely find in Amsterdam, plus pad thai and beef noodle soup. Ask for it hot and it arrives hot.

  There is barely any seating here, because this is food to carry home. That suits the Rozengracht, one of the main shopping streets in the Jordaan, where half the neighbourhood walks past on the way back from work. Dong Son also has a second location in the Houthavens.

  Vegetarian dishes are on the menu, and the kitchen will work around what you can and cannot eat. Call ahead or walk in, and say how much heat you want.
