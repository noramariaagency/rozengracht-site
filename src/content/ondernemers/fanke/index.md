tekst_en: |
  Antonios and Marios met in Amsterdam through the football club AEK Athens, and this spring they opened Fanke at Rozengracht 91.

  Fanke is a Greek deli with a coffee bar inside it. The signature item is bougatsa from Thessaloniki, lighter than filo pastry, savoury with feta and mizithra or sweet with vanilla cream. The menu also runs to freddo cappuccino and freddo espresso, fresh fruit juices, olive oil and cheeses, much of it from the region around Volos.

  This is not their first place in the city. They opened the coffee bar Alpha before this, and they bring that experience of the Amsterdam food scene to the Rozengracht. Their starting point is regional produce and Greek food without the familiar cliches.

  You can take your coffee away or pick up a slice of bougatsa, and part of the shelf comes home with you. What is in stock shifts with what arrives from Greece.

  Walk in at Rozengracht 91 in Amsterdam for a freddo and something from the counter.
