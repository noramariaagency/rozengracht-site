  At Rozengracht 88 a workout lasts fifty minutes, alternating between the treadmill and the dumbbell stations.

  Rush runs group classes in three shapes: one for the full body, one for the lower body and one for the upper body. The format stays the same and the emphasis shifts from week to week. Coaches including Sylvana, Zsazsa, Bart, Floortje and Mauricio talk the group through the rounds. Rush is a Trainmore concept, and members holding a Black or Red Label get credits every four weeks to join in.

  The studio sits on the Rozengracht in Amsterdam's Jordaan, on a spot that already housed a gym before, which makes it the nearest place to train for a lot of people in the neighbourhood.

  There are changing rooms and showers, payment is by card, and booking goes through rushstudio.nl or Eversports. If you want to try it first, buy a single class.
