tekst_en: |
  Wegewijs, Rozengracht 32, Amsterdam

  Fa. H. Wegewijs has been at this spot on the Rozengracht since 1912.

  More than a hundred years ago, the Wegewijs family started a butter and cheese trade on the Rozengracht. What remains is a delicatessen shop that still operates the same way: nothing pre-packaged, nothing pre-cut. Everything is freshly prepared. The range includes more than fifty varieties of foreign cheese and over a hundred Dutch types, from young aged to mature farmhouse cheese that has been ripening on the shelf.

  Wegewijs is a sandwich shop as well as a cheese merchant. You can stop by for a filled roll at lunch or pick up a piece of cheese for dinner. The shop has become a regular stop for Jordaan locals and visitors who know that good cheese doesn't have to come off a supermarket shelf.

  Find them at Rozengracht 32. Call ahead on +31 20 624 4093.
