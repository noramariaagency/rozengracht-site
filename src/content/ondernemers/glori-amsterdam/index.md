tekst_en: |
  Glori Amsterdam at Rozengracht 194 is the restaurant of two chefs who both came from far away: one from Argentina, one from Italy. Together, on the Rozengracht, they make something that is more than either of them individually.

  The menu moves between Argentine, Spanish, and Italian cooking, but not in the obvious way. Wednesday through Saturday the kitchen is open for dinner. On Sundays, Glori runs an Italian pasta lunch. Changing exhibitions from local artists hang throughout the restaurant: the walls shift with the seasons.

  On the Rozengracht, between the Jordaan and the rest of the city, Glori has become the restaurant that locals know well and visitors from further afield make a point of finding.

  Open Wednesday–Saturday for dinner, Sunday pasta lunch. Rozengracht 194, Amsterdam. More at gloriamsterdam.com or via info@gloriamsterdam.com.
