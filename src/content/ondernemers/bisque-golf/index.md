tekst_en: |
  Ido, Marc, and Cheng opened the golf store Amsterdam actually needed in February 2023.

  Bisque Golf at Rozengracht 17 was founded by three Amsterdam retailers who know what a good store looks like: Ido de Voos (founder of concept store 90 Square Meters), Marc van Dodewaard (founder of Sneaker District), and Cheng Liem Li. They shared a love for golf and a conviction that the sport deserved a different kind of shop: not a dusty pro shop, but somewhere you'd walk into even if you don't play.

  The store interior, designed by Barde vanVoltt, reflects that. So does the brand selection: Malbon, Local Rule, Students Golf, and Bisque's own label alongside more classic golf apparel. The range is deliberately curated and tight. Bisque is built for a new generation of golfers who want to look good on and off the course.

  Find Bisque Golf at Rozengracht 17, open Wednesday through Sunday.
