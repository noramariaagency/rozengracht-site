  Nico Hafkamp knows his regulars by name, and you notice it the moment you step into the Gouden Florijn.

Draught beer, a row of specialty beers, whisky, coffee and Dutch bar snacks: spicy bitterballen, a plate of cheese, salmon on toast, a bowl of vegetable soup. Four screens hang on the walls, so on match days the room fills up with people from the Jordaan who mostly know each other already.
