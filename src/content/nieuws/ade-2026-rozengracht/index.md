tekst_en: |
  From 21 to 25 October, Amsterdam belongs to the Amsterdam Dance Event for five days. The 2026 edition is a milestone: ADE turns thirty. Hundreds of events are spread across the city, with thousands of artists and an enormous range of electronic music.

  The Rozengracht takes part too. This street runs along the southern edge of the Jordaan, a ten-minute walk from Central Station and a few minutes from the Melkweg and Paradiso. Over the coming weeks, keep an eye on the websites and social channels of the record shops Velvet Music and In Deep 'N Dance for their ADE programming and activities. Chin Chin Club and Bar Theo will also be going all out during these days.

  Whether you are coming to Amsterdam specifically for ADE, looking for new music, or simply want to be part of the atmosphere that runs through the city that week, there is good reason to walk down the Rozengracht as well.

  Five days of music, energy and a city in full swing. It promises to be another fine edition.
