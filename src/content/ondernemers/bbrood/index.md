tekst_en: |
  Bbrood bakes fresh sourdough every morning at Rozengracht 180-182. This has been happening for over ten years based on one principle: good bread, as little waste as possible.

  The bakery centres on sourdough and offers a daily range of loaves and rolls. What doesn't sell on the day goes out the next morning at half price: the "waste no more" approach Bbrood has applied from the start. Bbrood has multiple locations in Amsterdam and also invests in bakeries in East Africa, training local bakers.

  On the Rozengracht, where you can grab an early breakfast or pick up a loaf on your way somewhere, Bbrood fits in naturally.

  Open Monday–Saturday 7:30–17:00. Rozengracht 180-182, Amsterdam. Contact: rozengracht@bbrood.nl or 020 261 9172.
