tekst_en: |
  Mondzorggilde is at number 68-70 on the Rozengracht and opens four days a week, for people from the neighbourhood and for patients who travel further because they have known this practice for years.

  The team of dentists, dental hygienists and assistants gets the everyday work right: check-ups, fillings and cleaning. Heavier treatment is available here too, from periodontal care and crowns and bridges to implants and braces. There is specific attention for oral care for older patients. An emergency number covers the hours the practice is closed.

  Locally the practice is still known as Leuke Tandarts, which translates roughly as "The Nice Dentist", and that name is not a marketing line. The approach is built on giving patients the time and the explanation they need.

  Mondzorggilde is not a single practice but a national group of nearly twenty dental practices. The one on the Rozengracht is the Amsterdam location, and new patients can register through Mondzorggilde. This stretch of the Rozengracht holds a lot of care and services together, which makes the practice easy to reach from the whole surrounding area.

  Open Monday to Thursday 8:30–17:00 (closed Friday). Rozengracht 68-70, Amsterdam. Phone 020 626 0951.
