tekst_en: |
  Jan Winter started selling cigars and ink on the Rozengracht in 1914, two things that were, at the time, essential to anyone who had something to say.

  Gebroeders Winter grew into one of Amsterdam's best-known stationery and office supply shops. The shop survived two world wars, multiple ownerships, and sweeping changes to the city, but stayed on the Rozengracht 62. After a difficult period in 2014, it was given a new start and has continued under its original name ever since.

  More than 110 years on the same street: Gebroeders Winter is one of the rare Amsterdam shops you can still find exactly where it began.
